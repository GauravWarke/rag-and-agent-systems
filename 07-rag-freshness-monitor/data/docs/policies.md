# Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase if the product has not been activated. Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days back to the original payment method.

# Data Retention Policy
Account data is retained for 90 days after account deletion. After the 90 day window the data is permanently purged from all systems, including backups.

# Security Incident Policy
Any suspected security breach must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery. Customer passwords are always hashed and are never stored in plaintext.

# Acceptable Use Policy
Accounts found sending spam or otherwise violating the acceptable use policy may be suspended without prior notice.
